Frequently Asked

How much does a website cost in Bangladesh?

It depends on what the website has to do. A professional company site is a different project from an e-commerce platform with payments and inventory. You'll get a fixed written quote after one conversation — free, no obligation.

Can you build an app that works with bKash / Nagad?

Yes — bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz and international card gateways are all standard integrations here.

What is an AI chatbot and does my business actually need one?

It's software that answers customer questions automatically — on your website, WhatsApp or Messenger — using your own product and policy information. If your team answers the same 20 questions every day, it usually pays for itself.

Why open source instead of Microsoft/Oracle/SAP-style licensed software?

The licensed giants are excellent — and priced for multinationals. Open-source equivalents deliver the same core capability with zero license fees and no vendor lock-in. You own everything that's built.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes. Every project ships with a support arrangement in writing — updates, backups, monitoring, and a real human to call.

Can our data stay fully inside our own servers?

Yes. Everything — including AI — can run entirely on infrastructure you own, with nothing leaving your network. This matters for organizations handling sensitive data, and it's a specialty here.
