  <h2 class="t-h3" id="what-is">What the framework covers</h2>
  <p class="t-body">The Australian Signals Directorate publishes eight cybersecurity mitigation strategies for small business. They are the baseline Australian insurers, government suppliers and enterprise customers increasingly measure against.</p>
  <h2 class="t-h3" id="ml1">Maturity level 1: regular backups and restore testing</h2>
  <p class="t-body">Maturity level 1 is the sensible floor for most small businesses. For regular backups, it expects data, applications and settings to be backed up according to criticality, kept securely, and restore testing performed as part of disaster recovery planning.</p>
  <p class="t-body">Backup restore testing matters because a backup that has never been restored is a guess, not a backup. Test a real restore on isolated hardware, time it, and record the result.</p>
  <h2 class="t-h3" id="controls">Immutable backup and ransomware recovery</h2>
  <p class="t-body">Level 1 also covers multi-factor authentication on online services, application control that blocks unverified macros, separate admin accounts, and patching critical online services within 48 hours. Backups are stored so ordinary accounts cannot change or delete them, and an immutable backup protects against ransomware recovery scenarios.</p>
  <p class="t-body">Document each control with a one-page summary and the evidence behind it. Reassess at least once a year. Keep the written plan with your vendor contracts and show it to your auditor or insurer on request.</p>
