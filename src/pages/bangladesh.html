Why work with me

An AI developer in Dhaka who stays after launch

AI automation in Bangladesh usually starts small: a chatbot that answers customers at night, a workflow that copies orders from a form into a sheet and sends an SMS, or a report that builds itself every morning. I build these with n8n workflow automation and AI steps only where they genuinely help, so the system keeps working when you are busy.

I have spent 2.5 years building software for up to five organisations, including government-scale systems where downtime is an incident, not a metric. The same habits come to every project here: a written plan before work starts, a weekly demo, and backups that are tested so nothing is lost.

The same work goes to clients in the US and Australia. Businesses in Bangladesh get it in Bangla or English, with meetings in Dhaka when that is easier than a call.
