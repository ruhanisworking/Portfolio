Story

An AI automation engineer from Dhaka

Ruhan grew up in Bangladesh and studied Computer Science and Engineering at KUET, where his thesis was on verifiable search over encrypted cloud data. He graduated in 2024 with a CGPA of 3.72 and the Dean's Award.

Since May 2024 he has been a Programmer at the Institute of Information & Communication Technology (IICT), BUET, where he is the lead engineer on national exam, results and logistics platforms: full-stack work, DevOps, database administration and security hardening. In 2025 he also led a full-stack team for a US-based software company, remotely.

Through ruhan.bd he builds private AI assistants, n8n workflow automation, websites and custom business software for businesses in Bangladesh, the US and Australia. His rule: the client owns the system, the data and the machines it runs on.

How he works is simple: a written plan and a fixed price before anything starts, a short demo every week, and handover notes at the end, so the client's own team can run what was built. Most of the systems run on proven open-source software on the client's own server, with tested backups and monitoring from day one.
