Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026. Questions: {{site.email}}.

This website

ruhan.bd has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The only thing it stores in your browser is your language choice, if you change it. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is served through Cloudflare. Both see standard request data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

The contact form does not send anything to a server. It opens a pre-filled email in your own mail app, and you decide whether to send it. Mail you send me is used only to reply to you and to do the work you ask for.

Hermes and Commander (my own tools)

Hermes is a personal assistant I run on my own server, and Commander is my private dashboard. They are used only by me, for my own accounts. Hermes uses Google OAuth to read and write my own Google Calendar: it creates my meetings with Google Meet links, adds reminders and lists my schedule.

  • It accesses only the Google account that signed in, which is mine.
  • Calendar data is used only to run my schedule and is shown only to me. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Access tokens are stored on my own server, and access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
