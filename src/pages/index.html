05 · Questions

Self-hosted AI & remote work, answered

Do you work with clients in the US and Australia?
Yes — all of my client work is remote. My day in Dhaka (GMT+6) overlaps the Australian working day, and for US clients I work while you sleep and hand over by your morning. Calls fit your morning or evening.
What does "private" or "self-hosted" mean for my business?
The software runs on a computer or server you control — in your office or your own cloud account — instead of on someone else's platform. Your data stays there, and you never pay per-seat licence fees.
How do you price a project?
After the free assessment you get a fixed price in writing for the setup. Monitoring, backups and support can continue on a simple monthly plan — or I hand everything over to your team.
Will you sign an NDA and work to HIPAA or Essential Eight?
Yes, I sign NDAs. I build to those control sets and give you the evidence — configurations, test results, reports. The formal assessment or certification is done by your auditor.
Can you work with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?
Yes. Private AI, automation and backups connect to the tools you already use — nothing needs to be replaced just to get started.
